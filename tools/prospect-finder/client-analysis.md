## Sweet Spot Profile

Your **ideal client** appears to be:

### Primary Sweet Spot
- **Healthcare organization** (insurer, provider, digital health) with complex digital needs
- **National reach**, 500+ employees
- **Budget**: $150K-$500K annually
- **Needs**: Member/patient engagement, content strategy, digital transformation

### Secondary Sweet Spot
- **Higher education institution** (R1 university or selective college)
- **Alumni magazine or advancement marketing**
- **Multi-stakeholder digital ecosystem**
- **Budget**: $100K-$300K annually

### Tertiary Sweet Spot
- **Health/wellness publisher** or enthusiast media brand
- **Print-to-digital transformation**
- **Content-heavy, audience-focused**
- **Budget**: $75K-$200K annually

## Unique Strengths Indicated

1. **Regulated industries** - Healthcare, energy, education (complex compliance)
